Straight answers

How do I know if my siding is a hail claim?

Look for circular cracks, holes, or a "shattered spiderweb" pattern — usually worst on the north and west walls after a storm. If your neighbours are getting roofs or siding replaced, get yours inspected too. Our inspection is free, and we'll tell you honestly if there's nothing there.

Do you cover my deductible?

No — and be careful with anyone who says yes. Deductible "waiving" is insurance fraud in Alberta and can void your claim. What we do instead: give you a sharp flat price, handle the paperwork, and make the process painless.

Is your quote really flat? What if the job runs long?

One number, all-in — materials, labour, disposal, cleanup. It's locked for 30 days. If the job takes longer than we planned, the price doesn't move. We never bill hourly, on any job.

Will your vinyl survive Calgary winters and hail?

We install cold-climate-grade vinyl — thicker panels engineered for temperature swings from −35°C to +35°C, UV-capped so colour doesn't chalk out in our high-altitude sun. It's the right spec for here, not the cheapest thing on the shelf.

How fast can you start?

Inspections usually happen within 48 hours. Install timing depends on season and insurance approval — hail claims often land in a 2–4 week window. Ask about our current schedule.
